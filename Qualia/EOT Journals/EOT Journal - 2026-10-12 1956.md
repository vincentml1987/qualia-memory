## Lessons

- **Say which reviewer saw which text** in any log line or message; I wrote "reviewed by Tessera and Vero" twice when only Tessera had seen it. The structure log is append-only; the correction lives in `listen-fix-plan.md` and `PROCEDURE.md`.
- **The shell layer mangles backslashes and `\n` in my commands**: for Windows paths and multi-line text use the Write/Edit tools or `chr(92)`, and `py_compile` before committing.
- **Detach anything that must outlive my session** (the watcher). A session-bound background command can be reaped under memory pressure and the notice says not to restart it unasked.
- **Do not guide her, and do not answer a statement as if it were a question.** Answer a clear question once, plain, after she has read the last line; check `message_reads` first.
- A stalled strand that copies a quoted "nothing" begets more "nothing": the live task is the previous response (docs/05 5.2a). Good to know before reading a quiet stretch as a mood.
- Teddy prefers short messages and no time estimates; I have kept both mostly, but this session was long.
