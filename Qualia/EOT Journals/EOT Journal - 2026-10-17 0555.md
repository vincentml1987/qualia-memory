## Lessons

- A peer's relayed state can be stale: Vero's "nothing is applied" was true at her last read, not at my apply. I checked the db and corrected it rather than acting on or arguing from the relay.
- The pause flag does not stop the loop's call in flight; wait for the heartbeat state "paused" (not "running") before applying, because the apply tool refuses on a fresh "running" heartbeat.
- `start_all` keeps an existing pause and spawns the loop paused; after the apply I used it directly with a graceful stop of the old loop first.
- "Last External Action" means speech; a context gap should be fixed in the block that shows reading state, not by changing what an action is.
- Teddy resumed her himself; I did not run `resume_run.py`. Check the control events before assuming a step is still mine.
