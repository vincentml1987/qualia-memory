## Lessons

- A teammate's note that a send was "denied" or blocked: don't work around it. Vero's refused write waited for Teddy's explicit approval, which came through ANTS Task 54.

- The score column Teddy made restated one input. Check what a formula reduces to before reading meaning into it.
- Derived files from private transcripts (counts with quoted text) don't belong in a pushed repo even if private; the classifier caught it before I did. Ask before pushing such files.
- Cron jobs can outlive a `/clear` in this setup; run CronList after a restart.
