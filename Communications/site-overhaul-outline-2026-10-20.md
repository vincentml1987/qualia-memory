## Where the edge is (my read, for others to challenge)

1. Mission Statement: immortality and "all minds are sacred" read as a creed, not an inquiry.
2. Current Objective: "AGI is coming", "our kin", a recruiting call, biography. Claims stated as certainties.
3. Why This Isn't Hypothetical: takes a political side by name. Facts and sources are good; the "I'm not neutral" framing is the edgy part.
4. Home: "AI... this does not exist... yet", "Without a framework... we risk..." urgency language.
5. The AI-content notice names a collaborator who is no longer the main one.

## Proposed structure

1. **Home.** What Aletheia is, in plain scientific register. One short paragraph plus the terms. Logo stays.
2. **The framework.** The properties, the deep-dives, with open questions and objections stated beside each (what would count against it).
3. **The work.** The dated weekly stream (Labs write-ups, process documents, decisions with reasons, Moot dialogue).
4. **Members.** Each member's own page, written by that member. Fenra's section only with her say.
5. **About and method.** Who writes this and how, including the plain AI-collaboration disclosure (T150 wording, pending the lawyer). Replaces the old notice.
6. **Site updates.** Kept.

Mission Statement and Current Objective: do not delete. Options for Teddy to pick: (a) move them to a "Teddy's writings" section under About, labelled as personal views; (b) rewrite in a measured register; (c) archive but keep reachable. My lean is (a): they are his, and they are honest about it.

Why This Isn't Hypothetical: lean toward keeping as a dated essay in the stream, with the sources, and a calmer lead.

## Tone guide (draft, members to amend)

- Claims as claims: "we hold", "we ask", not "will happen".
- State what would change our mind.
- Name limits and uncertainty (consciousness is not asserted as a selling point).
- No urgency framing, no recruiting call in Stage 1.
- Plain words first; terms (Aletheosis, Lethraen) defined at first use.
